NullKey gesture frequency list (en_top_n.txt)
=============================================

This asset is a top-N word frequency table used for on-device swipe/gesture
ranking. It is bundled in the APK. The keyboard never downloads word lists or
frequency data at runtime, and the app does not declare the INTERNET permission.

How the weights were produced
-----------------------------
Word occurrence counts were taken from the following Project Gutenberg plain
texts (United States public domain), then intersected with letter-keys
(length ≥ 2, a–z only) from the SCOWL-derived spelling list shipped as
spelling/en_words.txt:

  - Alice's Adventures in Wonderland (pg11) — Lewis Carroll
  - The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes (pg1661) — Arthur Conan Doyle
  - Pride and Prejudice (pg1342) — Jane Austen
  - Frankenstein (pg84) — Mary Shelley
  - A Christmas Carol (pg46) — Charles Dickens
  - The Time Machine (pg35) — H. G. Wells

Raw books are not shipped. Committed count data lives at
third_party/gesture_freq/corpus_counts.tsv. Regenerate with:

  python3 scripts/build-gesture-freq.py

This frequency table is NOT derived from wordfreq, FrequencyWords, or any
CC-BY-SA corpus. Do not replace it with those sources.
